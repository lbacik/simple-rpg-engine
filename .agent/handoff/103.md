## Design notes for the continuer

- Flag-eligible set deliberately includes `combat-start` (its body is opening narration, a Scenario-text block) and excludes all pure engine events even when they carry names (travel, combat-round/end, enemy-defeated, inventory, state-change, test). This makes both acceptance sentences hold simultaneously under ADR 0004's definition of "engine-generated entries".
- Journal page chrome (`Back to the game`, `N entries.`, `Nothing has happened yet.`, list loading/position strings) is still hardcoded English — intentionally left out of scope; entry labels/messages (the spec's "Interface Text in the Journal") already follow the catalogue.
