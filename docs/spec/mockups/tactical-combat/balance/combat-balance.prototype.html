Tactical Combat balance harness PROTOTYPE · issue #83

Question. Do the agreed intent, initiative and action rules give the existing Character Templates changing, understandable choices in one- and two-Enemy Combats, and what numeric tuning is needed before implementation?

The rules follow docs/spec/mechanics.md (Rounds, Character actions, Enemy Intents). Characters use their effective Attributes with starting Equipment; Enemies are the shipped ones. The solver plays perfectly: it knows every probability and maximises the chance of winning (with a small preference for Health left and for shorter fights). Its value for each action is "your chance to win if you take this action now and play perfectly afterwards". Tuning presets change only the listed numbers.
